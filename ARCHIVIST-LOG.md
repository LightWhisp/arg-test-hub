# THE ARCHIVIST — voice guide and log entries

Voice derived from *All Multiples Of Six* by girania (AO3), chapter 1.

---

## Who she actually is

**Josephine "Pepper" Weber.** German. A **backend database engineer** at a mid-sized data
management firm — server architecture, storage pipelines, repairing integrations left
behind by developers who quit. She got here because a burst pipe killed her workstation,
she bought a refurbished laptop, and it had a folder on it called `misc_backup_6` that
would not delete.

### Why she can read only the dead letters

**Not a coincidence about her origin — a consequence of what she is.**

The dead letters are the only part of his tongue that is **pure value with the meaning
stripped out**. Six. Ninety. Nine hundred. Nothing else.

She is a database engineer. She reads schemas, types and values. She does not read prose.
So the emptied marks are the only thing in the language that behaves like data, and
therefore the only thing she can read at all.

**Her gift and her blindness are the same faculty.** She can read what has been emptied of
meaning, and she cannot read meaning. That is why 894 lands: she reads *void being* as a
**type** — a value in a schema — when it is an **identifier**. Same faculty, both times.

Two things she already says and never hears:

- In **042** she writes *"cleared the field, and kept the key."* She uses the word **key**,
  in her own professional register, about an encoding — and does not follow it.
- In **090** she notes she has counted in sixes since `misc_backup_6`, the file that took
  her. The number that took her is the number he emptied. She never asks why.

**Never reference the title of her story.** She did not write it and cannot know it.


### The correction this forces

**She is not a naturalist.** Everything currently written for her — *specimen*, *genus*,
*taxonomy* — is the wrong register. She is a systems person. Her native vocabulary is:

> record · row · field · column · schema · type · instance · index · query · transfer ·
> integrity · corruption · permissions · orphaned · malformed · null · commit · flag

This makes the finale **sharper**, not just more accurate. Rewrite the 894 explanation in
the beat sheet from:

> *To a taxonomist, "void being" is a genus.*

to:

> **To a database engineer, "void being" is a type — not an instance.** She has him filed
> under a class name and never once checks whether it is an identifier. Programmers do
> this every day of their lives.

### How she writes

- **Terse.** Sentences stop when the information stops.
- **Dry to the point of contempt**, and funniest when understating. *"Well. That's irritating."*
- **Hates noise, attention, and being helped.** Kindness creates expectations.
- **Irritated by anomalies**, not frightened by them. Bad data hygiene offends her.
- **One sardonic beat per entry, maximum.** Usually the last line.
- **German surfaces only when she is alone and annoyed.** Once every ten entries at most.
- First person. Never *she*. Never the word *Archivist*. Never addresses a reader.

### 090 — the reserved one

Ninety is one of his numerals. Spend it here:

> **entry 090.** Count of recovered fragments stands at ninety.
> I notice I have been numbering in sixes since the first file I ever opened, which was
> called `misc_backup_6`, and that I have never once chosen to stop. Habit, probably.
> Structure is comfortable.
> Ninety is a good round number. Moving on.

She states the count. She states her own numbering habit. She names the exact number that
is one of his dead letters, calls it *a good round number*, and moves on. The audience
will lose their minds and she will never know why.

## Optional: Lord-X in the margins

The fic establishes that **X shares her mind during the day and comments in her head**,
rendered in full-width brackets. She reports to him willingly. So his voice can legitimately
appear in the archive as marginalia — a second character on the site for almost no cost,
and it does not touch the Void/Archivist separation, because X is not Void.

The version I would build toward:

> **entry 894.** … Closing the query. Transmitting to X.
>
> ［Ｔｈａｔ ｉｓ ｎｏｔ ｈｉｓ ｔｙｐｅ， Ｐｅｐｐｅｒ．］

He was *there*. He asked *"some form of void being?"* in Act 0 and got *"I am….I am."* in
reply. He has always known. And he lets her close the file anyway, because someone who
adores watching things struggle and persevere does not hand over the answer.

**The risk:** worded any more helpfully than that, it hands the community the finale
early. It must read as amusement, never as a hint — and it must land *after* she has
already transmitted and closed, so there is no version where she corrects herself.

This also pulls the *does Lord-X know she failed?* thread out of the "reserved for the
game" list and pays it off inside the ARG. Your call whether that is a gain or a spend.
